About

Economist and Data & AI Specialist with experience designing and building end-to-end data systems, automation pipelines, and analytics solutions. My work focuses on transforming raw, unstructured data into structured, actionable insights that support real-world decision-making, combining Python, SQL, BI tools, and modern data technologies with practical problem-solving.

Beyond technical implementation, I work with data as an organizational asset: data quality practices, process structuring, and alignment between producers and consumers. I am particularly interested in data governance, automation, and analytics, building systems that are not only technically sound but reliable, consistent, and usable across teams.
